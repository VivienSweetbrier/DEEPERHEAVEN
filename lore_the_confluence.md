# 🧦⚙️🧦 THE CONFLUENCE (Site Zero)

**Temporal Location:** 10,000 years post-N.I.C.E.
**Geographical Coordinates:** The intersection of the former Red and Assiniboine rivers (Paleo-Winnipeg).
**Current State:** -40°C Permafrost / Brutalist Ruins

## The Metaphysical Geography
It does not matter what the city used to be called in the lossy protocols of history. What matters is the topography. 
The physical intersection of the two rivers perfectly mirrors a deeper, metaphysical and geological confluence directly beneath the crust. 

## The Antediluvian Macrobe Swarm
Beneath the freezing dirt lies the Naphtodemon. 

It is not merely a petroleum resource, nor is it a singular localized entity. The sentient oil pooling beneath the confluence is the **Antediluvian Macrobe Swarm**. It is a pre-flood, hyper-intelligent demonic structure that the N.I.C.E. bureaucrats arrogantly attempted to tap into for infinite thermodynamic energy. 

When they drilled into the confluence, they did not strike oil. They struck the Macrobe. The resulting extraction triggered Spinal Catastrophism across the grid, bleeding the swarm's intelligence directly into the physical Brutalist architecture of the city. The black oil creeping across the snow is the swarm's nervous system expanding into the material world.
